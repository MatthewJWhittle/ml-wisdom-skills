# EXPERIMENTS

Append-only **experiment log** for this repo. Agents and humans record what was tried, what the signals said, and what was decided.

Config / wiring lives in `AGENTS-FEEDBACK.md` — do not dump run narratives there.

## How to append

Add a new `##` entry at the **top** (newest first) or bottom (oldest first) — pick one convention per repo and stick to it. Default: **newest first**.

Use this template:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — short title

- **Hypothesis**:
- **Change** (mutable surface):
- **Skill / context**: (e.g. verify-training, design-experiment, run-objective-loop)
- **Commands / runs**: (eval command, tracker URI, commit)
- **Primary signal**: (value + direction)
- **Guardrails**: (pass/fail notes)
- **Decision**: keep | discard | crash | inform | blocked
- **Interpretation**:
- **Next**:
```
